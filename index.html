MCU / CRD Guidelines - 2026
1. Eligibility for Recognition as Research Supervisor *
An applicant shall be eligible for recognition under one of the categories specified in Clauses 1, 2 or 3, and shall in every case satisfy the departmental prerequisite.
1.1 Category A — Professors and Associate Professors (Existing Faculty) (i) A Ph.D. degree in the concerned discipline or in a cognate discipline approved by the Research Board; and (ii) A minimum of two (2) years of PG teaching experience, during the service, till date. (iii) A minimum of five (5) post-Ph.D. recognized publications. (iv) A minimum of three (3) years of experience post Ph.D.
1.2 Category B — Assistant Professors (Entry Level) (i) A Ph.D. degree in the concerned discipline or in a cognate discipline approved by the Research Board; (ii) A minimum of five (5) years of MCU Experience; (iii) A minimum of two (2) years of PG teaching experience, during the service, till date. (iv) A minimum of three (3) post-Ph.D. recognized publications. (v) A minimum of three (3) years of experience post-Ph.D.
1.3 Category C — Relaxed Route for Post-Doctoral Experience (i) A Ph.D. degree in the concerned discipline or in a cognate discipline approved by the Research Board; (ii) Post-Doctoral Experience of not less than two (2) years; (iii) A minimum of two (2) Post-Ph.D. recognized publications, of which at least one (1) shall arise out of, or be attributable to, the post-doctoral work; (iv) A minimum of two (2) years of MCU Experience; and (v) 1 year of PG Teaching Experience.
Explanation: The relaxation under this Clause is available once to an applicant and is confined to the reduction of the MCU Experience requirement from five years to two years, and of the publication requirement from three to two, as prescribed above. No further relaxation shall be granted under this Clause.
NB: A faculty member seeking recognition as a Ph.D. Research Guide shall have at least three years of service remaining before the date of retirement.
